# CrossFire competition artwork

`arena-contact-sheet.png` is an original 1536 × 1024 photographic atlas generated with the built-in imagegen tool on 8 October 2026. It is illustrative event artwork, not photography of actual CrossFire participants. The six cells are displayed through SVG viewports in `src/pages/LandingPage.tsx`; the original atlas is kept intact.

Final generation prompt:

> Use case: website photographic texture atlas. Create ONE coordinated photographic contact sheet, landscape 1536x1024, a precise 3-column by 2-row grid of six edge-to-edge SQUARE photos, no gaps, no borders, absolutely NO text, letters, numbers, logos or watermarks. Every photo looks like cinematic high contrast editorial black and white photography, subtle cool cyan undertones and tiny selective orange accents, deep black backgrounds, for an Indian college talent championship. TOP LEFT: two Indian college students one young man wearing glasses and one young woman, facing stage, thoughtful competitive expressions, at a quiz table with a small red buzzer foreground. TOP MIDDLE: expressive Indian male college debater in dark blazer holding microphone, side portrait gesturing with hand. TOP RIGHT: extreme close-up of student's hand holding paintbrush creating a poster, orange pigment on textured paper. BOTTOM LEFT: overhead documentary shot of three students with backpacks racing down concrete campus stairs on a treasure hunt. BOTTOM MIDDLE: confident Indian female student in elegant long black modest gown at a college runway competition, full body side profile, dramatic stage backlights. BOTTOM RIGHT: closeup hands holding phone horizontally recording a busy Indian campus at sunset, phone screen clearly shows same scene. Keep primary subjects central in each square and leave some dark space near edges for separate HTML titles. Exact aligned equal grid, each photo completely contained in its square. Rich photographic detail and natural anatomy.

The existing CrossFire logos, SAGS crest, campus illustration and PDFs remain project assets. Anton and Barlow Condensed are served locally from `public/fonts`, with their OFL licenses alongside them.
