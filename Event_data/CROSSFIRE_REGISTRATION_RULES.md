## ⚠️ IMPORTANT RULES & CONSTRAINTS

### Maximum Participation
- **Rule 1:** A student can participate in **maximum 2 events**
- **Exception:** If participating in a team event (Quiz/Treasure Hunt), student can participate in 1 additional individual event
- **Examples:**
  - ✅ Quiz (2 members) + Ramp Walk (solo) = Allowed (2 events)
  - ✅ Ramp Walk (solo) + Debate (solo) = Allowed (2 events)
  - ❌ Quiz (2 members) + Treasure Hunt (3 members) = NOT Allowed (Team size exceeds limits)

### Event-Specific Rules

#### Quiz
- ✅ Team of exactly 2 members
- ✅ Written round selection (top 6 teams advance)
- ✅ Final round at campus on event day
- ⚠️ 6 teams only qualify for final round
- Prize: ₹6,000 to ₹1,500 per team

#### Ramp Walk
- ✅ Individual participation only
- ✅ Duration: 2 minutes per participant
- ✅ Live performance on stage
- ✅ Judge decision final
- Prize: ₹3,000 to ₹1,000

#### Reels
- ✅ Individual participation only
- ✅ Pre-recorded video (30-60 seconds)
- ✅ Submission deadline: November 14, 2026
- ✅ Filmed on campus on event day
- ✅ File formats: MP4, MOV (Max 50MB)
- Prize: ₹3,000 to ₹1,000

#### Debate
- ✅ Individual participation only
- ✅ Topic communicated via WhatsApp/SMS on event morning
- ✅ Preparation time: 15 minutes
- ✅ Speaking time: Opening 2 mins + Rebuttal 1 min
- ✅ Judge decision final
- Prize: ₹4,000 to ₹1,000

#### Poster Making
- ✅ Individual participation only
- ✅ Theme announced at event start
- ✅ On-campus creation during event
- ✅ Physical or digital submission
- ✅ Judge decision final
- Prize: ₹4,000 to ₹1,000

#### Treasure Hunt
- ✅ Team of exactly 3 members
- ✅ Multi-station puzzle hunt across campus
- ✅ Scoring: Speed (50%) + Accuracy (50%)
- ✅ Judge decision final
- Prize: ₹3,000 to ₹1,000

### General Rules
- ✅ **No Entry Fee:** All events are completely free
- ✅ **Judge Decision Final:** All judge decisions are final and binding
- ✅ **Valid ID:** Proof of school/college required
- ✅ **Age Verification:** Must be 16-18 years old
- ✅ **Attendance:** Must attend on November 15, 2026
